HAASEWALD

The family map of the land

Android phones

Download the app (9 MB)
Version 2.10, built November 14, 2026.
  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded Haasewald.apk (from the notification, or Files > Downloads).
  2. The first time, Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser or Files app: tap Settings, switch Allow from this source on, go back, and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details > Install anyway. (It is a private family app, not from the Play Store.)
  4. Open Haasewald and allow location. To see the family, open the menu: My land > Family location sharing > Join with a code I was given, and enter the family code you were sent.
To update, download again from this page and install over the old one: your markers, trails and saved maps stay.

iPhone and iPad

Use the web version: same map, parcels, contours, land trails and family locations.

Open the web version
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open the web version.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.
  3. Allow location. Tap the family button (top right), enter your name, Join with a code I was given, and switch sharing on.
On iPhone your location is shared only while Haasewald is open on the screen; Apple does not let web pages run in the background.

Property lines, aerial photos and contours come from public sources and are not a legal survey.
